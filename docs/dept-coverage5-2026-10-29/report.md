## 3. Incident: robots.txt on a redirect destination (found this round)

The scope crawler checked robots.txt for the URL it requested but not for the host a redirect led to. `www.springer.com/journal/<id>` redirects to `link.springer.com`, which disallows automated access. Fixed in `aims_scope.py`; `robots_audit.py` re-checked every page URL kept in the scope files of rounds 2-5 (761 hosts) against its own host's robots.txt and purged 36 (round 2), 40 (round 3), 238 (rounds 4 and 5) entries: 233 `link.springer.com`, 3 `pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov`, 2 `ocean.kisti.re.kr`, and one each on two small journal hosts. Four inserted journals carry a `link.springer.com` page among their recorded evidence (*Cellular and Molecular Bioengineering*, *Biomass Conversion and Biorefinery*, *Journal of Signal Processing Systems*, *e+i Elektrotechnik und Informationstechnik*); each rests on two other kinds of agreeing evidence without it, so eligibility stands, nothing is rolled back, and the page is to be disregarded (details in `robots-redirect-incident.md`). Production audit rows are never edited.
