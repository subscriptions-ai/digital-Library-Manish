# Incident: robots.txt was checked on the first host but not on the redirect destination

**What happened.** `aims_scope.py` checked robots.txt for the URL it was asked to fetch, then followed HTTP redirects. `https://www.springer.com/journal/<id>` (allowed) redirects to `https://link.springer.com/journal/<id>`, whose robots.txt says `User-agent: * Disallow: /`. The page content was therefore read from a host that disallows automated access, in the previous round (201 Springer journal pages) and, through the same redirect, in earlier rounds (31–33 entries each). Three other entries had landed on `pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov` and two on `ocean.kisti.re.kr`; two further hosts (`ijetjournal.org`, `www.journals-sathyabama.com`) were also found to disallow the pages kept.

**Fix.** `aims_scope.get()` now checks robots.txt for the final URL whenever a redirect leaves the requested host and discards the content if it is disallowed. `scripts/dept-coverage/robots_audit.py` checks every page URL kept in a scope file against the robots.txt of its own host and purges the entry (status `robots-disallowed (redirect destination)`, hits and pages emptied). It ran on the scope files of rounds 2, 3, 4 and 5: 36, 40, 238 and 238 entries purged. No decision in this round rests on a purged page. The pre-audit files are kept locally (`scope.pre-audit.json`), not committed.

**Inserted journals affected.** Four of the 93 inserted journals carry a `link.springer.com` page among their recorded evidence: *Cellular and Molecular Bioengineering* (title + catalogue), *Biomass Conversion and Biorefinery* (title + topics + site), *Journal of Signal Processing Systems* (title + topics), *e+i Elektrotechnik und Informationstechnik* (title + catalogue). Each still has two other kinds of agreeing evidence without that page, so eligibility stands and nothing is rolled back; the page is to be disregarded as evidence. The production audit rows keep the original evidence as written (audit rows are never edited).

**Consequence for coverage.** Springer-hosted journals cannot be scope-checked automatically: their aims-and-scope pages are disallowed. Only other evidence kinds (title, society list, catalogue subject, OpenAlex topics) or a person can resolve them.
