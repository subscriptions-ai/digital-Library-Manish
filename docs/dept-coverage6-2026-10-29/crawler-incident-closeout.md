# Crawler incident closeout (robots.txt on redirect destinations)

Companion to `docs/dept-coverage5-2026-10-29/robots-redirect-incident.md`. Closed before any further crawling.

## 1. Access rules now cover every hop

`aims_scope.get_ex()` follows redirects by hand: robots.txt is consulted before the first request and before **every** redirect target (any host, any path), at most 5 hops, a non-http target is refused, and a disallowed URL is never requested and returns no content. The status recorded is `robots-disallowed` (first URL) or `robots-disallowed (redirect destination)`, with the chain of URLs (no content). Tests (`scripts/dept-coverage/test_crawler_redirects.py`, 12 cases, three local servers acting as hosts): allowed → allowed host; allowed → disallowed path on another host; allowed → disallowed host; chain of three allowed hops; chain allowed → allowed → disallowed (stops before the last hop, the disallowed path is never requested); two-host chain ending in a disallowed path; same-host redirect to a disallowed path; a directly disallowed URL; redirect loop and a six-hop chain (bounded); non-http redirect target; and that the page record keeps the status and chain but no content.

## 2. Collected evidence re-audited and purged

`robots_audit.py` checked every page URL kept in the website-evidence files of rounds 2-5 and the homepage-recovery file against the robots.txt of the page's own host (761 hosts; 67 more for the recovery file): 36 (round 2), 40 (round 3), 238 (rounds 4 and 5) and 4 (recovery) entries were purged. The local pre-audit copies, which still held the discarded page text, were deleted. The historical chain of intermediate hops was not recorded for pages collected before the fix (only the final URL), so intermediate hops of those pages cannot be re-checked; that limitation is accepted because every purge decision used the final URL and no decision in rounds 5-6 rests on a page whose final host disallows access.

## 3. The four affected inserted journals

A scan of **every committed manifest** against each page host's robots.txt (71 hosts) finds exactly four inserted journals with a disallowed page among their recorded evidence, all on `link.springer.com`. Each was revalidated on admissible evidence only (retained: title, catalogue records, OpenAlex topics, publisher deposits). Independence: a title term, an NLM Catalog record, a publisher grouping list and OpenAlex topics are different kinds; OpenAlex and Crossref repeat the same upstream DOIs, so publication evidence is counted once, from Crossref's own deposits.
