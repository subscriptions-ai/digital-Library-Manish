# STM Digital Library - Official Platform Walkthrough

**Target Audience:** Prospective Clients (Institutions, Corporates, Students)
**Tone:** Informative, Transparent, and Professional.

---

### **Step 1: Showcasing the Digital Library**
**Visual:** 
- The video starts on the STM Digital Library Landing Page.
- Slowly scroll down to highlight the massive content collection: Books, Periodicals, Case Reports, and Educational Videos.
- Highlight the beautiful UI, the domain-specific sections, and the advanced search features available right on the homepage.

**Voiceover (VO):**
> "Welcome to the STM Digital Library, a centralized and highly advanced academic repository. Our platform hosts an extensive, world-class collection of books, periodicals, case reports, and educational videos, curated to empower modern learning ecosystems with seamless digital access."

### **Step 2: Requesting a Demo (The Right Way)**
**Visual:** 
- Cursor moves to the top navigation bar and clicks the "Request Demo" button.
- A clean, detailed form pops up. The cursor highlights the 'Request Type' dropdown menu.
- Show the dropdown options clearly: "Institution", "Student", and "Corporate".
- The user selects an option, fills in details like Name, Institutional Email, and WhatsApp number, and clicks "Submit".

**Voiceover (VO):**
> "To experience the platform firsthand, simply click on 'Request Demo'. 
>
> Our platform caters to everyone. In the form, under 'Request Type', you can accurately identify yourself by choosing whether you are a Student, representing an Institution, or a Corporate entity. Fill out your official email and contact details, and click Submit. It’s that easy."

---

### **Step 3: Initial Email Confirmation**
**Visual:** 
- Switch to the user's Email Inbox.
- A new email arrives: "Your Demo Session Request has been received".
- Open the email to show the clean formatting and the "Next Steps" listed.

**Voiceover (VO):**
> "The moment you submit the form, our system immediately sends a confirmation email to your inbox. This email assures you that your request has safely reached our expert team, who will review your details and reach out within 24 hours to schedule a personalized walkthrough."

---

### **Step 4: Receiving the Quotation & Registration Process**
**Visual:** 
- Fast forward visually. A new email arrives: "Your Quotation from STM Library".
- User opens the email, clicks "View & Accept Quotation".
- The Quotation page loads, showing the customized plan. The user accepts it and is taken to the Registration screen.
- User enters a password.
- **Crucial Step:** A prompt asks for a 6-digit OTP. User checks their email, copies the OTP, pastes it, and clicks Verify. A success message appears.

**Voiceover (VO):**
> "After your personalized demo and finalization of requirements, our system sends you a customized digital quotation. You can review the pricing and subscription details right from your email. 
>
> Upon accepting the quotation, you proceed to Account Registration. Enter a secure password, and for enhanced security, the system will send a 6-digit OTP to your email. Enter this OTP to verify your identity and instantly activate your Institutional Account."

---

### **Step 5: Exploring the Institutional Dashboard**
**Visual:** 
- The user logs in and lands on the Institutional Dashboard.
- Briefly show the top overview cards (Active Users, Storage, Subscriptions).
- Navigate to "My Subscriptions" to show active plans.
- Navigate to "Content Library". Click on a book to show the Secure PDF viewer working smoothly inside the browser.

**Voiceover (VO):**
> "Once logged in, you gain full access to your powerful Institutional Dashboard. 
>
> Here, you have complete transparency over your active subscriptions and platform analytics. By navigating to the 'Content Library', you can browse the premium resources assigned to your institution. Clicking on any book or video opens it directly in our built-in, highly secure viewer—offering a flawless reading and viewing experience without ever needing to download files."

---

### **Step 6: Adding Users & Managing Access**
**Visual:** 
- The cursor clicks on "Student Management" (or Users section) in the left sidebar.
- Click "Add Student". Enter a student's Name and Email. 
- Show how the platform automatically sends a 'Welcome' email with auto-generated login credentials to that specific student.
- Next, highlight the "Bulk Import" button and show a CSV being uploaded for adding hundreds of students simultaneously.

**Voiceover (VO):**
> "Managing your users is highly intuitive. Go to the 'Student Management' section to add new members to your institution's digital library. 
>
> When you add a student's name and email, the system automatically dispatches a welcome email to them, containing their secure login credentials. For larger groups, simply use our Bulk Import feature. Upload a standard CSV file, and you can instantly onboard hundreds of students or faculty members in a single click."
